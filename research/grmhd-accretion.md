## Overview

Matter falling onto a black hole or neutron star forms an accretion disk, and how it sheds angular momentum decides how fast it accretes and how jets are launched. This was my senior thesis project with Prof. Rahul Kashyap at IIT Bombay.

## What I did

- Used **AthenaK** to simulate compact-object accretion disks across length scales, **testing scale invariance**.
- Studied the mechanism and effects of **3-D magneto-rotational instability** on angular momentum transport.
- Connected the simulations to observations across multiple scales to gain insight into **disk and jet structure**.
- Used X-ray binaries to test predictions of **strong gravity near rotating black holes**.
