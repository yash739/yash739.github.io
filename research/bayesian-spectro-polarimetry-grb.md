## Overview

This is my MSc thesis, supervised by Prof. Varun Bhalerao: *Spectro-Polarimetric Analysis of the Inverse Compton Scenario in GRB260226A using AstroSat-CZTI, Fermi-GBM and Fermi-LAT Data with the Multi-Mission Maximum Likelihood Framework (3ML)*. The idea is to test emission models for gamma-ray bursts by fitting spectra and polarization together, instead of treating them separately.

## What I did

I led the spectro-polarimetric analysis of GRB260226A. Using AstroSat-CZTI together with Fermi-GBM and Fermi-LAT, I built joint fits in 3ML and compared them with what Two-Zone Inverse Compton Emission predicts.

Alongside that, I worked on GRB 230812B. I wrote time-resolved spectral fitting scripts and prompt-emission light curves, and used the thermal (blackbody) component to estimate the photospheric radius. I also modelled its multi-band afterglow, using MCMC sampling of afterglowpy models to constrain the jet and circumburst parameters from the full set of light curves.

I also reported joint-polarization measurements of GRBs 161218B and 170202B, which were seen by both CZTI and POLAR.
