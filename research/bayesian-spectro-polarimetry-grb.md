## Overview

This is my MSc thesis, supervised by Prof. Varun Bhalerao: *Spectro-Polarimetric Analysis of the Inverse Compton Scenario in GRB260226A using AstroSat-CZTI, Fermi-GBM and Fermi-LAT Data with the Multi-Mission Maximum Likelihood Framework (3ML)*. The idea is to test emission models for gamma-ray bursts by fitting spectra and polarization together, instead of treating them separately.

## What I did

- **Led the spectro-polarimetric analysis of GRB260226A** with AstroSat-CZTI, building joint GBM, LAT and CZTI spectro-polarimetric fits in 3ML and comparing them with the predictions of **Two-Zone Inverse Compton Emission**.
- Wrote **time-resolved spectral fitting scripts** and prompt-emission light curves for GRB 230812B, estimating the **photospheric radius** from the thermal (blackbody) component.
- Modelled the **multi-band afterglow** of GRB 230812B with **MCMC sampling of afterglowpy models**, constraining jet and circumburst parameters from the full set of light curves.
- Reported joint-polarization measurements of GRBs 161218B and 170202B, which were co-detected by CZTI and POLAR.
