## Overview

A summer data-science internship at SatSure Analytics on estimating depth and elevation from satellite imagery with deep learning, aimed at construction monitoring.

## What I did

I implemented RAFT-Stereo, a neural network, to replace classical stereo-satellite pipelines for estimating elevation from stereo image pairs, and used Meta's DINOv2 transformer to get depth from single satellite images.

I benchmarked both for construction-monitoring tasks against the open-source Google Open Buildings dataset, and built a comparison suite to measure the deep-learning approaches against ground-truth LiDAR data.
