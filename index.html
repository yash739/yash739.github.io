Yashowardhan Rai

I study high-energy astrophysics and multi-messenger science: how gamma-ray bursts radiate, what neutron star mergers tell us about dense matter, and how matter falls onto compact objects.

MSc thesis Spectro-polarimetric analysis of the inverse Compton scenario in GRB260226A using AstroSat-CZTI, Fermi-GBM and Fermi-LAT data with the Multi-Mission Maximum Likelihood Framework (3ML).

About

I am doing a B.Tech. in Engineering Physics (2022–26) followed by an MSc in Physics with a specialisation in Astrophysics (2026–27) at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay. I have been a student member of the LIGO India Science Collaboration since 2025.

My work sits between observation and modelling: fitting real GRB data with Bayesian frameworks, simulating radiative transfer and accretion flows, and training machine-learning models for physics applications.

High energy astrophysics. Compact objects, neutron star equation of state, simulations of accretion disks and jets.

Multi-messenger science. Gravitational waves; modelling of radio, X-ray and nuclear transients.

Research

GRB spectro-polarimetry

Prof. Varun Bhalerao · IIT Bombay · Dec 2023 – present

Time- and energy-resolved joint spectral and polarimetric fits of GRBs in 3ML. Contributed to the redevelopment of polarpy into PolPy (now part of 3ML) and built an injection-recovery pipeline for the upcoming Daksha polarimeter.

GRBsAstroSat-CZTI3ML

Non-equilibrium ammonia modelling

Dr. Thushara Pillai · MIT Haystack · May 2025 – present

Using the 3-D non-LTE radiative transfer code Magritte to synthetically image ammonia hyperfine inversion lines in cold proto-stellar clouds and investigate the hyperfine intensity anomaly. Code on GitHub.

Radiative transferISM

Likelihood-free gravitational-wave inference

Prof. Michael Coughlin · University of Minnesota · May 2025 – present

Using AMPLFI for likelihood-free parameter estimation of neutron star mergers, extending IMRPhenom binary-black-hole models to account for the neutron star equation of state.

Gravitational wavesMachine learning

GRMHD of compact object accretion

Prof. Rahul Kashyap · IIT Bombay · Aug 2025 – present

Simulating accretion disks with AthenaK across length scales; studying 3-D magneto-rotational instability and angular momentum transport, and connecting disk and jet structure to X-ray binary observations.

GRMHDAccretion

Compton polarimetry in a segmented detector

Prof. Varun Bhalerao · Aug – Dec 2024

Modernised legacy IDL pipelines for Geant4 simulations of CZTI into Python, implementing Compton double-event analysis, and wrote a simplified Compton-scattering simulator with rejection sampling and vector rotations.

Geant4Polarimetry

Lunar chemical abundances from X-ray fluorescence

Inter-IIT Tech Meet, first place · Oct 2024 – Apr 2025

Developed a novel algorithm on Chandrayaan-2 CLASS data to detect solar flares from XRF line strengths and made the first high-resolution map of XRF line ratios, identifying compositional groups.

Chandrayaan-2X-ray

Machine learning for intelligent reflective surfaces

Prof. Anshuman Kumar · IIT Bombay · Aug – Dec 2024

Implemented array-factor and Fourier far-field models and the Gerchberg–Saxton algorithm, then trained an autoencoder alternative with lower time complexity for real-time phase retrieval.

PyTorchInverse design

Deep learning for satellite elevation estimation

SatSure Analytics · May – Aug 2026

Implemented RAFT-Stereo for elevation from stereo satellite pairs and used Meta's DINOv2 for single-image depth, benchmarking both against LiDAR ground truth and the Google Open Buildings dataset.
